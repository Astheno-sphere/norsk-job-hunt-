# The lenses and the red team

These are methods adopted from real practitioners and applied by Claude. A tick from a lens is structured analysis, not a real recruiter's approval. Do not over-trust it.

## Constructive lenses (build the house)

### Raahul: written voice
- Concrete, mid-thought, picturable. Test: can the reader see it?
- One vivid deal beats three labels ("I brought Møre og Romsdal fylkeskommune to the table to co-design a seminar" beats "partners include a county, a studio and a rail firm").
- Failure signal: text that feels artificial or bloated. Tiger's own flag on the first VARD letter.

### Anna Papalia: interview persona
- Every letter should seed the interview: leave one line the interviewer must ask about.
- Plan the answer to that line before sending.

### Leanne Carter: insider positioning
- Certainty principle: safe beats impressive.
- Positioning beats experience. Hold the employer's world centre-stage; your background enters as the answer.
- Kill the "why a trainee?" doubt hard, not softly.
- The hidden job market is reached through people, not portals.

## Destructive pass (kick the door)

### Red team
- Question: where does a skeptical Norwegian HR screener or hiring manager throw this out?
- Score 0 to 100, list filters in order of lethality, list what does not earn its place, list what survives.
- For high-stakes applications, run it cold: a separate agent that has not seen the friendly history.
- Honest ceiling: no application passes a red team determined to fail it. The goal is to be failed only on structural risks (work authorization, Norwegian level, a hard credential), never on craft.

## Order of operations
Lenses first, then red team, then a consistency check of letter against the CV being sent.

## STARZZ modes
- Mode 1, commercial outcome: growth toward the Sport1 acquisition (EUR 27M). For BD and partnership roles.
- Mode 2, sourcing story: supplier qualification, spec compliance, quality accountability for gear worn on national TV. For logistics, procurement, quality roles.
- Pick one mode per application.
