## PART 5: ANIMA WRITING SYSTEM

### Architecture core
Open with L2 pain, not company intro. One story per letter: problem + I + number + lesson. First 90 days as a thinking approach (who Tiger talks to first, what each person knows the other doesn't, what that conversation produces), never a task list. Proposed solution in the opening cluster. Five clusters max. Close with a specific outcome and mental image. Decode the real question beneath the surface. Consequence over credential, always. Trailer not summary; value earned before gap named; never perform.

### Emotional reality (three layers, decode before writing)
L1 = operational failure mode. L2 = personal professional cost to the hiring manager when it breaks. L3 = emotional need beneath the hire (trust, relief, confidence). The letter must land on L2 and L3, not just L1.

### The five psychological clusters (visible in every full application)
1. **Feel understood:** open with L2 pain (personal cost to the hiring manager), not company intro; proposed solution.
2. **See the solution:** combination as answer + hyper-specific research detail + open loop.
3. **Believe the evidence:** flowing narrative + numbers alone + resistance moment + mirror sentence.
4. **Trust the person:** positive anchor + gaps confident + Norwegian calibrated.
5. **Know what next:** research table + first 90 days as a thinking approach + inevitable close with a specific mental image.

**Pain-first brief pattern** (proven in the Oda "Keeping the evening run on time" brief): open each section from the employer's daily reality, then bring Tiger in as the answer. Section titles = their problem, then his answer.

**Altitude rule:** tier-2 applications (trainee, coordinator) get a short letter (about 270 words), not the full five-cluster arc. The full arc is for strategic roles.

### Hard constraints
- One page hard limit for operational/coordinator roles. No exceptions.
- Full applications 500 words max. Future notes 250 to 350 words. Reading time 45 to 65 seconds.
- Repetition scan before sending: any word appearing 3+ times gets replaced. Common offenders: operational, coordination, process, ensure, leverage, utilize.
- Gap cluster rule: never more than 2 consecutive gaps. Structure: strength anchor + gap 1 + gap 2 + strength anchor + gap 3 (Norwegian).
- Anxiety killer: kill one named fear per letter.
- Three fatigue checks before sending: combination cluster too long; more than 2 consecutive gaps without positive anchor; first 90 days reads as a task list.

### Banned language (absolute)
Em dashes. "I can learn quickly." "Willing to learn." "We" in achievement stories. Badmouthing employers. Salary first. "Available immediately." Apple-washing (excited, perfect fit, love to join, passionate about). "I am writing to [company] specifically because." Consultant vocabulary: cross-functional coordination, operationalise, operational complexity, pre-engagement mapping, constraint-based planning, stakeholder conflict management, leverage. No exclamation marks. No horizontal lines. Bold sparingly.

### Key formulas
- **Recruiter reader rule:** one placement identity sentence: "supply chain and operations profile spanning [domain 1], [domain 2] and [domain 3] systems." Single CRM category.
- **Combination signal (Janteloven):** "It is an unusual combination: [elements]. It came together through specific choices, not by accident." Never rank against the room.
- **Modernisation signal:** "Most coordinators keep processes running. I map where manual work is heaviest and build something that reduces it. Your client keeps those tools after [date]."
- **Norwegian gap calibration:** English-working-language role = not a gap. Norwegian required = name it directly, early, after value established.
- **Execution vs systems balance:** "execution" is the anchor word; operator tone beats consultant tone. Ban "structuring flows," "mapping constraints," "framework thinking" as openers.
- **Human anchor rule:** one micro-human anchor inside the proof paragraph. Lived constraint reality in one sentence, not a story.
- **CV vs letter rule:** CV states the credential; letter deploys its consequence in the reader's world. Never restate.
- **Conversational presence:** sounds like Tiger talking across the table. Plain language: every sentence passes the 3-second test.
- **Persuasion moves:** specificity signal, mirror sentence, open loop, inevitable close.

### Three voices and precedence
- **Raahul** = written engine. Concrete, mid-thought. Test: can the reader picture it? Positioning move: most candidates come with one discipline; state where you sit, let the reader conclude.
- **Anna Papalia** = interview persona. Four styles. Easy questions are the real test; answer in 2 to 3 seconds; stories beat facts; structure not script; never accept on the spot.
- **Leanne Carter** = insider strategist. Certainty principle: hires die where doubt enters; safe beats impressive; positioning beats experience; unverifiable is the enemy; lead from the reader's pain, not from "I".
- "Anna texture" = a separate writing device (one human moment per evidence cluster), not the Papalia interview persona.
- These are methods applied by Claude, not real sign-offs. The red team is the destructive pass that runs after them. See `lenses.md`.

### Interview psychology (v3.5)
First 90 seconds decide. Easy questions are the real test: answer in 2 to 3 seconds, not 8. Stories beat facts. Why-this-role: connect their problem to your capability, never flatter. Salary: never speak first, ask their band; if forced, ONE number plus value. Closing question: "What does success look like in the first 90 days, in your view as manager?" Follow-up references a specific conversation moment. Intro = positioning + through-line + right now. Achievement = problem + I + number + lesson. Weakness = real gap + self-awareness + mitigation + target. Stress = admit real + story + thinking AND action. Panel = one conversation distributed. Pause before accepting any offer.

### Rejection replies
Gap rejection: acknowledge directly, offer something no-strings, one clean feedback ask. Volume rejection: lift hiring-manager guilt first, then one company-specific insight. Anti-Tiger principle: stay in researcher mode after rejection; name their pain, never reflect the company back at them.

### Strategic hire rule (strategic roles only)
Map profile to where the company is GOING. Frame research assets as a preview of what Tiger would build FOR THEM. Position as the solution to a future problem they are already naming.
