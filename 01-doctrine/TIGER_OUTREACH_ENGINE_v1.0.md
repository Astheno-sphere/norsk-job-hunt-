# TIGER_OUTREACH_ENGINE_v1.0.md
## The Weekly Warm-Channel Operating System
## Companion to TIGER_ANIMA_v5.0_UNIFIED (built June 2026)

---

## WHY THIS EXISTS

150+ cold applications produced an interview rate under 4%. Every win arrived warm. Leanne's doctrine: outreach is how the industry is designed to work. Recruiters build talent pools and the first call goes to whoever made contact before the job posted; up to 80% of senior roles are never advertised. The engine's job: manufacture five warm conversations a week, on purpose, instead of waiting for the next Janani. The metric is CONVERSATIONS STARTED, never applications sent.

---

## THE WEEKLY CADENCE (core tier: about 3 hours)

### MONDAY: Target Selection (30 min)
Pick exactly 5 targets for the week. Never more. Mix:
- 2 NEW COLD-WARM: a recruiter with relevant mandates, or a hiring manager near a live posting, or an operator with no posting (hidden market).
- 2 REACTIVATIONS: existing warm contacts gone quiet (`04-pipeline/contacts.md` is the inventory).
- 1 FOLLOW-UP: anyone at their day-3/4 or day-10 trigger.
Triage applies: English/soft-Norwegian, coordinator+, target geography. A target that fails triage does not get a slot.

### TUESDAY to THURSDAY: Send (60 to 90 min total)
One to two messages a day, never batched into one spam-feeling burst. Every message passes the THREE-PASS AUDIT before sending (Leanne: where does doubt enter? Raahul: can they picture it? Anna: does it survive a follow-up question?).

### FRIDAY: Sweep + Log (30 min)
- Fire any due follow-ups (day 3 to 4 nudge; day about 10 second touch WITH NEW VALUE; then park, never chase a third time).
- Update the ledger.
- Count the week: sends, replies, conversations, calls booked.

### CONTENT LEG: One post per week (45 to 60 min, any day)
Proven format (Decision Decay / battery-sequencing): counterintuitive finding + hyper-specific Norwegian detail (a road number, a port, a regulation date) + honest "I was wrong about X" moment + question close. Each post is bait for the outreach leg: within 48 hours, check who reacted or commented; the relevant ones become next Monday's targets. A post that produces two DM-able engagers has done its job regardless of impressions.

### EXPANSION TIER (+2 hrs if available)
+3 targets (8 total), or a second post, or one deep research-asset move (a mini-map for one specific company). Never expand by lowering target quality.

## THE THREE MESSAGE ARCHETYPES (Raahul voice, all under 120 words)

### ARCHETYPE 1: Recruiter talent-pool intro
> Hi [Name],
> I saw you place [domain] roles across [region]. Putting myself on your radar before the next mandate lands.
> I am a supply chain and operations profile spanning offshore logistics, technical procurement and cross-border compliance systems. MSc Sustainable Energy Logistics at HiMolde (2026), four years across DACH, India and UAE. At my current role audit cycles went from three days to eight hours; EUR 8M in approvals moved through the process I built.
> Based in [city], selectively exploring roles across Norway. Working language English; Norwegian early conversational, targeting B1 this year.
> If a relevant mandate comes up I would value a short conversation. CV on request or via my profile.
> Arshad

Rules: one placement identity sentence; one number; scarcity posture; honest language line; no attachment unless invited.

### ARCHETYPE 2: Hiring manager near a live posting (apply + direct note same day)
> Hi [Name],
> I applied for the [role] today, and one line in the posting made me write to you directly: [quote their exact operational detail].
> That is the problem I have spent the last [X] doing. At [company], [one-sentence story with the number]. [One lesson line if it fits.]
> [One honest gap line if structurally relevant.]
> I would value fifteen minutes on what the first ninety days actually need to fix. Would this week or next suit you?
> Arshad

Live precedent: the Sperton reply. Posting detail (serial numbers), matching story (Astorvia), honest line, call ask.

### ARCHETYPE 3: Value-first operator outreach (no posting; the hidden 80%)
> Hi [Name],
> [One sentence proving real knowledge of their operation: a route, a regulation hitting them, a constraint.]
> Before I engage with any new environment I map it first. I did this for [comparable company/sector]: [link to ONE matched asset]. For [their company] the equivalent would be [one concrete sentence].
> Not pitching anything. I am finishing an MSc in Sustainable Energy Logistics at HiMolde and work in exactly this space. If the map is useful, I would enjoy comparing notes for twenty minutes.
> Arshad

Rules: ONE asset, matched to their pain, pulled not pushed. No job ask in message one.

### Trainee note (Nov 2026)
For trainee programmes, swap "four years across DACH, India and UAE" for the trainee logic in ANIMA Part 10 (STARZZ 2 years, START Molde, MSc).

### FOLLOW-UP TOUCHES
- Day 3 to 4 nudge: two sentences, reference the original hook, one new micro-detail. Never "just bumping this."
- Day about 10 second touch: must carry NEW value (a relevant article, a development in their space, a small addition to the map). Then park. Parked is not dead; they re-enter the pool when something real changes.

### BANNED IN ALL OUTREACH
Everything banned in ANIMA Part 5, plus: "looking for my next challenge," "just reaching out," "I hope this finds you well" as an opener, "pick your brain," any message over 150 words, any message with two asks, any message that could have been sent to ten companies unchanged.

## THE LEDGER

Kept in `04-pipeline/outreach-ledger.md`.

| Name | Company / Channel | Hook used | Sent | Next trigger | Status |

Status values: SENT, REPLIED, CONVERSATION, CALL, APPLICATION/INTRO, PARKED.
Weekly review (Anna): what does the reply rate say about the hooks, and which archetype is converting? Monthly review (Leanne): is the interview rate moving toward 15 to 25%? If reply rate is under 15% after 3 weeks, rebuild the hooks, do not raise the volume.

## OPERATING PRINCIPLES

- Warm > recruiter inbound > cold portal. Five good beats fifty cold.
- Conversations started is the metric. Applications are a byproduct.
- Every message survives one follow-up question in a live room (truth over clever).
- Scarcity posture always: Tiger has a pipeline, research that companies engage with, and a finishing MSc. Carry that reality; never perform need.
- The post feeds the outreach; the outreach feeds the pipeline; the pipeline feeds the posture.
- Polishing finished assets is displacement. When tempted, send one message instead.
