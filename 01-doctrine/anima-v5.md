# ANIMA v5.0 (condensed)

Master doctrine for all application and correspondence output. Unified from v4.2 and v4.3.

## Architecture
- Open with the reader's L2 pain, never a company intro.
- One story: problem + I + number + lesson.
- Five clusters max. Full application about 500 words; future notes 250 to 350.
- CV: each role opens with a problem story; bullets lead with the system or problem, not a verb.
- Research assets matched to reader pain, one or two per application, never stacked.
- Consequence over credential. Trailer, not summary. Value earned before a gap is named.

## Five-stage psychological arc
1. Feel understood: their L2 pain plus the proposed solution.
2. See the solution: the combination as answer, one hyper-specific research detail, an open loop.
3. Believe the evidence: flowing narrative, numbers, a resistance moment, a mirror sentence.
4. Trust the person: positive anchor, gaps stated with confidence, Norwegian level calibrated.
5. Know what next: research table, first 90 days as a thinking approach (not an action list), a close that leaves a specific mental image.

## Scannability
- Bold sparingly, no horizontal lines, numbers breathe alone.
- Reading time 45 to 65 seconds.
- Repetition scan: flag any word used more than 3 times.
- Gap cluster rule: never more than 2 gaps in a row; break with a positive anchor.
- Norwegian plain language: every sentence passes the 3-second test.
- Ban corporate vocabulary: cross-functional coordination, operationalise, leverage, synergy.

## Scarcity
- Never "available immediately". Use the city alone or "selectively exploring roles in Norway".

## Locked phrasings
| Item | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Norwegian level (EN) | early conversational, studying daily, targeting B1 by end of 2026 |
| Norwegian level (NO) | tidlig samtalenivå, studerer daglig, mål B1 innen utgangen av 2026 |
| SAP | completing S/4HANA MM and PS |
| Licence | Førerkort klasse B (India) |
| Person ID | Registrert i Folkeregisteret (norsk fødselsnummer) |
| BEM KEK | Pvt Ltd, never Pty |
| SubWallet | honest capped line only: conferences and merchandise |

## Banned
- Em dashes.
- "I can learn quickly", "we" in achievement stories, badmouthing employers, salary first, "available immediately".
- Apple-washing: excited, perfect fit, love to join, passionate about.
- German on CVs (removed entirely).
- MoonFit (deleted permanently).
- Exclamation marks.

## Titles and level
- Pitch titles at coordinator or operational level so the reader sees fit-for-level. MSc present but not looming.
- START Molde and LO-Studentene get their own visible sections. START Molde framed as a role held and worked in.

## Design and export
- ATS CVs: Calibri, single column, no ligatures, full https:// links.
- Briefs and presentations: Caladea display, Carlito body, navy with amber accent.
- Print to PDF via Chrome, never Word's export.
- Address swaps by region (Molde for Møre og Romsdal, Oslo for Oslo area, city only elsewhere). Actual addresses are not stored in this repo.
