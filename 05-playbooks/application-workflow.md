# Application workflow (harmonised Nov 2026)

Run when Tiger pastes a job. ANIMA builds, recruiter intel sharpens, red team attacks, Tiger decides.

## Stage A: Triage (2 minutes, before any writing)
1. Fill `templates/job-intake.md` from the posting, including the red-team variables.
2. Hard-mismatch check (`01-doctrine/hr-perspective.md`). Stated no-sponsorship, a credential Tiger lacks, or Norwegian fluency for phone/sales = SKIP, log reason, stop.
3. Verdict to Tiger in three lines: fit, the one real risk, apply or skip.

## Stage B: Channel (before the portal)
4. Warm route? Contact, alum, recruiter in `04-pipeline/contacts.md`.
5. Kontaktperson named in the ad? Draft a short call script or note: one fit question, one honest line, ask what matters most to them (NAV/EURES tactic). Answers feed the letter.

## Stage C: Build
6. Route the CV (`03-cvs/variants.md`) by `experience_cap`, `working_language`, `cv_language_risk`. Address by region.
7. Read the reader: their L2 pain in one sentence, in their task words.
8. Draft at the right altitude: trainee/coordinator about 270 words; strategic up to 500. One story, one STARZZ mode, one research asset, one commitment signal.
9. Constructive lenses: Raahul (picturable?), Anna (which line seeds the interview?), Leanne (their world centre-stage, doubt killed?).

## Stage D: Attack
10. Red team per `05-playbooks/red-team-protocol.md`. Cold agent (`.claude/agents/red-team.md`) for specialist, strategic, or any role Tiger marks high-stakes.
11. Apply only the P0 and P1 diffs. Re-run the mechanical sweep.

## Stage E: Ship
12. Render once (Chrome print to PDF). Native speaker check for any Norwegian text.
13. Log one row in `04-pipeline/applications.md`; set the follow-up date (ask in interview, else five working days, LinkedIn).

## Weekly engine (full spec: `01-doctrine/TIGER_OUTREACH_ENGINE_v1.0.md`, log in `04-pipeline/outreach-ledger.md`)
- Five warm targets Monday, sends Tue to Thu, sweep Friday, one post a week.
- Book T4 forklift. Register with temp agencies. Review funnel every Friday.
