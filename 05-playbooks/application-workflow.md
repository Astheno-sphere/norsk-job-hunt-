# Application workflow

Run for every posting. Fill `templates/job-intake.md` first.

1. **Fit check.** Hard mismatch from `01-doctrine/hr-perspective.md`? Skip and log the reason.
2. **Channel.** Is there a warm route (person, alum, recruiter)? If yes, that goes first, before the portal.
3. **Route the CV.** Pick the variant in `03-cvs/variants.md`. Lock the address by region.
4. **Read the reader.** Name their L2 pain in one sentence using their own task words.
5. **Draft.** ANIMA v5 architecture. One story, one STARZZ mode, one or two research assets.
6. **Lenses.** Raahul (picturable?), Anna (which line seeds the interview?), Leanne (their world centre-stage? why-a-trainee killed?).
7. **Red team.** Score, filters by lethality, what does not earn its place. Cold agent for high-stakes.
8. **Consistency.** Every role in the letter is on the CV. Dates match. Norwegian phrasing locked.
9. **Mechanical checks.** No em dashes, repetition scan, word count, full https:// links, banned phrases.
10. **Render once.** Chrome print to PDF. Native speaker check for Norwegian.
11. **Log.** One row in `04-pipeline/applications.md`.
