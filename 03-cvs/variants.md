# CV variants and routing

Pick the variant before writing the letter. The letter may only cite roles on the chosen CV.

| Variant | File | Shows | Send to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trainee (NO) | `trainee-no.md` | STARZZ only, Norwegian anchors, research light | Trainee programmes with an under-3-years expectation (VARD, Geminor, Cambi type) |
| Procurement trainee (NO) | trainee-no.md + Astorvia and BEM KEK entries | Procurement roles back in | Programmes whose experience cap counts only post-graduation years (OKEA type) |
| Full experience (EN) | `full-en.md` (to build) | All three roles, START Molde BD | English-working-language coordinator and commercial roles |
| Full experience (NO) | `full-no.md` (to build) | Bokmål master | Norwegian coordinator roles |
| Computational specialist (EN) | `specialist-en.md` (to build) | TRF leads, models, tools | AI-logistics, analyst, decision-support roles |

## Routing questions
1. Does the posting cap experience? Counted how (total or post-graduation)?
2. Which of the employer's task words do my roles answer best?
3. Which STARZZ mode fits?
4. Is the working language Norwegian or English?
5. Which region decides the address?
