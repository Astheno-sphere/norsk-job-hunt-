# Evolution of the search

The learning curve, in order. Each phase names what we believed, what broke it, and what we kept.

## Phase 1: Document polish (Mar to Apr 2026)
- Belief: a better CV gets interviews.
- Work: full CV diagnostic, scored 6.2/10 and iterated to 9.7/10. Headline built around Norwegian vocabulary (vareflyt, leveranse). One-page ceiling. Reverse-engineered cover letters for Norwegian logistics roles (Harald A. Møller Supply Chain Planner and others).
- Honesty correction that stuck: Astorvia was reframed from invented freight coordination to what it actually was, vendor and investor compliance frameworks for DACH datacentre procurement.
- Kept: ATS format, Norwegian operational vocabulary, problem-first bullets.

## Phase 2: Research assets and "study from the outside" (Apr to Jun 2026)
- Belief: show, don't claim. Build something for the employer.
- Work: Photoncycle supply chain graph, Veøy fleet optimisation, GSFleet diagnostic, ZNL battery landscape, Norsk datacentre logistics model. Jan Sagmo (ZNL) validated the battery graph publicly.
- Kept: the method is a named, repeatable asset. Always framed as outside-in product and market study, never as a validated operational finding.

## Phase 3: Funnel diagnosis (Jun 2026)
- Data: 150+ applications over four months, interview rate under 4% against a healthy 15 to 25%. Every pipeline win came through a warm channel.
- Finding: a channel problem, not a document problem. Binding constraints are no Norwegian employer anchor and sub-B1 Norwegian, confirmed by the Nordic Beverage Solutions rejection.
- Doctrine born: channel precedence (warm > recruiter > cold portal).
- Lenses adopted from real practitioners: Raahul (written voice), Anna Papalia (interview), Leanne Carter (positioning, certainty). Integrated into ANIMA v4.1.
- LinkedIn rebuilt through four export-review cycles and declared done. Do not reopen.

## Phase 4: ANIMA consolidation (Jul to Sep 2026)
- v4.2 and v4.3 unified into ANIMA v5.0 as the single master doctrine, paired with the Outreach Engine v1.0 for weekly cadence.
- v4.3 locks: Norwegian bokmål CVs by default, fødselsnummer never printed, licence phrasing with (India), region-swapped address, coordinator-level titles, START Molde and LO as visible Norwegian anchors, pain-first brief pattern.
- VARD Maritime Trainee 2027 (deadline 18.10.2026): first trainee CV, experience limited to STARZZ to fit the under-3-years expectation. Letter rebuilt after Tiger flagged the first draft as bloated and not in Raahul's voice. Course modules (Discrete Event Simulation, Capacity/Facility Planning, Scheduling) turned the production-rotation gap into a studied domain.
- Oda Driver Operations Manager: warm invite after a café conversation. Two honesty framings locked (leadership = team-building and training, not line management of 35 to 50 drivers; GSFleet = outside-in study).

## Phase 5: Trainee push and red team (Oct 2026)
- Roughly a dozen tailored applications: Deloitte, CHSnor, Microsoft, OKEA, Nordic Beverage Solutions, PwC, Oda, Anticimex, Subsea7, Star Information Systems, ECN, Geminor, Cambi, Nofir, Nortek, a hydropower trainee, SLB, OneSubsea.
- Red-team of the trainee CV: 52/100 for a Norwegian trainee programme, about 80 for an analyst or computational role. Mismatch, not weakness. See `01-doctrine/hr-perspective.md`.
- Key strategic tension, resolved: Tiger's computational work is genuinely rare for a master's student. But "can work in every section of supply chain" is the breadth problem renamed. Rarity converts only when legible and aimed. Decision: trainee CV stays humble; build a separate computational-specialist CV for AI-logistics, analyst and decision-support employers.
- CV routing learned the hard way: OKEA needed the procurement roles back (cap was post-graduation experience), Geminor needed STARZZ-only to stay consistent.
- Lenses clarified as methods applied by Claude, not real sign-offs. Red team is a structured skeptical pass, not an external panel.
- Thesis back to TRF (VERA-SNARK), published on GitHub and Streamlit. Can be reframed as information logistics for specialist roles.
- TRANSPLAN PhD proposal submitted track (deadline 1 Nov 2026).

## Phase 6: Repo as engine (Nov 2026, now)
- Moved everything into this repo to stop regenerating documents.
- Next: build the computational-specialist CV, run a cold red-team on it, restart the weekly warm-outreach engine, book T4 forklift.

## Lessons that compound
1. Channel beats document.
2. Aim beats breadth.
3. Honest framing of a gap beats hiding it, and only the structural risks (work authorization, Norwegian level) should ever be what fails an application.
4. One picturable proof beats three labels.
5. CV and letter must be one story.
